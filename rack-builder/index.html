Rack Builder

Rack Builder Help

Plan your 19-inch equipment racks. Lay out real gear and document every patch connection, all before you lift a single piece.

Racks

  • + Add rack creates a new rack. Type a name in its title bar.
  • Drag the title bar to move a rack around the workspace.
  • Drag the handle at the bottom of a rack to add or remove rack spaces (U). A rack can't shrink past the lowest device installed in it.
  • 🗑 in the title bar deletes the rack.

Adding gear

  • Search the gear library by name, or filter by category: Wireless, Audio, Power, FX or Misc.
  • Drag a device into a rack. Open spaces light up where it fits.
  • Half-rack devices can sit side by side in the same rack space.
  • Drag a placed device to move it within a rack or to a different rack. Its labels and notes move with it.

Labels, patching and notes

  • Hover over a device to see its patch info. Click the popup to edit it:
    • Add inputs and outputs with a description and where each one is patched to.
    • Rack drawers get a notes field to describe what's inside.
  • Label panels have a text field you can click and type in directly.
  • 🔌 Patch List shows every connection across all your racks in one table.

Removing gear

Drag a device back onto the sidebar, or click the × on it.

Undo

Click ↺ Undo or press Ctrl/Cmd + Z to reverse your last change.

Saving your work

  • Your layout saves automatically in this browser, so it'll be here when you come back on this device.
  • Export downloads your layout as a file you can keep, back up or send to someone.
  • Import opens a saved file. It replaces whatever is currently on the workspace.

Tip: drag the sidebar's edge to make it wider or narrower. Don't see your gear? Use the Request a device link at the bottom of the sidebar.

Best on a desktop or laptop with a mouse.

Rack Builder is designed for desktop screens. For the best experience, please switch to a larger display.
