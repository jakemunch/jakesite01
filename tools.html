Tools

Free planning tools for drummers, engineers, and anyone building out a rig. No sign-up required.

🥁 Drumkit Designer

Plan your kit from overhead. Lay out drums and cymbals at real-world sizes, try different setups, and save or share the design.

  • True-to-scale drums and cymbals with custom sizes and shell colors
  • Rotate bass drums and flip the whole kit for left-handed players
  • Export your layout to keep or send to a teacher or bandmate
Launch Drumkit Designer

🎛️ Rack Builder

Plan your 19-inch equipment racks. Lay out real gear and document every patch connection, all before you lift a single piece.

  • Drag-and-drop library of real studio, wireless, and power gear
  • Note inputs, outputs, and drawer contents on every device
  • See every connection across all your racks in one patch list
Launch Rack Builder

💻 Both tools are designed for desktop or laptop screens with a mouse. Your work saves automatically in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
